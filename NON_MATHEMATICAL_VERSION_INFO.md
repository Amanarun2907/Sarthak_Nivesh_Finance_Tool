## How the Formulas Were Handled

### **Process:**
1. ✓ Script scanned entire paper for mathematical equations
2. ✓ Identified equation sections by markers like "Equation 1:", "Equation 2:"
3. ✓ Detected formula patterns (=, +, -, x, /, parentheses)
4. ✓ Removed pure mathematical lines
5. ✓ Replaced with descriptive explanations
6. ✓ Kept ALL surrounding text, context, and explanations
7. ✓ Preserved ALL statistics, tables, and results

### **Detection Criteria:**
Mathematical lines were identified by:
- Lines starting with "Equation X:"
- Variable assignment patterns (X = Y)
- Mathematical operators in combination with parentheses
- Specific variable names (G_listing, R30, S_orch, etc.)
- Formula notation patterns

### **Replacement Strategy:**
- Short descriptive sentence explaining what was calculated
- Focus on WHAT is computed, not HOW
- Plain English instead of mathematical notation
- Preserves understanding without complexity
