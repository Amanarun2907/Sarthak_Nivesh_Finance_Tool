1. MATHEMATICAL EQUATIONS - MUCH CLEARER
=========================================

BEFORE (Example):
------------------
Gₗᵢₛₜ = (Pₗᵢₛₜ - Pᵢₛₛᵤₑ) / Pᵢₛₛᵤₑ × 100      (1)

AFTER (Example):
-----------------
Equation 1: Listing Gain

The listing gain represents the first-day return that investors would realize 
if they received allocation at the issue price and sold at the listing day 
closing price. Mathematically, listing gain Gₗᵢₛₜ is defined as:

Gₗᵢₛₜ = ((Pₗᵢₛₜ - Pᵢₛₛᵤₑ) / Pᵢₛₛᵤₑ) × 100

This formula computes the percentage return, with positive values indicating 
that the stock listed at a premium to its issue price, a favorable signal of 
market demand. For example, if an IPO has an issue price Pᵢₛₛᵤₑ of 100 rupees 
and lists at Pₗᵢₛₜ of 125 rupees, the listing gain equals ((125 - 100) / 100) × 
100 = 25 percent, indicating strong initial reception.

and real-world examples.

EXAMPLE 1: Sentiment Analysis Equation
----------------------------------------

BEFORE:
Sₛₑₙₜ(a) = 0.4 × VADERₐ + 0.3 × TextBlobₐ + 0.3 × Keywordₐ  (10)

AFTER:
We combine these three sentiment measures for article a through weighted 
averaging, yielding a composite sentiment score:

Sₛₑₙₜ(a) = 0.4 × VADERₐ + 0.3 × TextBlobₐ + 0.3 × Keywordₐ

VADER receives the highest weight at forty percent due to its superior 
performance in financial contexts. TextBlob and keyword approaches each 
receive thirty percent weight, providing diversity in the ensemble. This 
weighted combination ensures that no single method dominates the sentiment 
assessment, reducing the impact of individual method weaknesses while 
capturing the strengths of each approach.


EXAMPLE 2: Agent Weights Explanation
--------------------------------------

BEFORE:
Weights: wₚᵣᵢcₑ=0.20, wₘₐcᵣₒ=0.15, wₛₑₙₜ=0.20, wᵣᵢₛₖ=0.20, wᵢₚₒ=0.25

AFTER:
In our implementation, weights are assigned based on empirical validation on 
training data and domain expertise about the relative importance of different 
factors in IPO performance. Specifically, we assign:

wₚᵣᵢcₑ = 0.20 for the Price Movement Agent
wₘₐcᵣₒ = 0.15 for the Macroeconomic Agent
wₛₑₙₜ = 0.20 for the Sentiment Agent
wᵣᵢₛₖ = 0.20 for the Risk Agent
wᵢₚₒ = 0.25 for the IPO Intelligence Agent

The IPO Intelligence Agent receives the highest weight at twenty-five percent 
because it incorporates IPO-specific signals like QIB participation that prior 
research has identified as strongly predictive. Price Movement and Sentiment 
agents each receive twenty percent weight reflecting the importance of 
technical momentum and market sentiment. The Risk Agent also receives twenty 
percent weight as risk management is critical for retail investors. The 
Macroeconomic Agent receives fifteen percent weight as it provides important 
context but is somewhat less specific to individual IPO outcomes.
